Who we are

Valkyrie Strategic Solutions is a veteran-owned Florida company that provides strategic management, data analytics, and operational support to local, state, and federal agencies and the prime contractors who serve them. We pair rigorous analysis with disciplined execution to improve planning, align resources, strengthen procurement, and support informed decision-making. Every engagement concludes with training, so client teams can sustain the results independently.

Core competencies

Strategic management and planningStrategic plans, performance frameworks, and program evaluation.
Analytics and reportingData analysis, dashboards, and recurring reports that support evidence-based decisions.
Territory and resource alignmentAnalysis-driven alignment of territories, workload, and resources.
Operations and schedulingWorkflow design, capacity planning, and scheduling support.
Procurement supportSupport for procurement planning, documentation, and tracking.
Training and developmentInstructional programs for sales, field, and operations personnel.
Property and asset managementAsset inventories, lease and contract calendars, and maintenance planning.
Program and project managementMulti-year planning, milestone tracking, and budget oversight.

Representative experience

  • National territory alignment, oncology and pathology. Physician-level analysis supporting the evaluation and balancing of territories across a national footprint.
  • Root cause analysis. Systematic investigation of data and process discrepancies, with findings and corrective actions documented.
  • Documentation and validation. Documented methodology and multi-step validation, including record reconciliation, so results are accurate, repeatable, and auditable.

Why Valkyrie

  • Integrity. Objective, accurate findings, reported plainly.
  • Communication. Consistent, transparent reporting on scope, schedule, and cost.
  • Experience. Senior-level expertise applied directly to every engagement.
  • After-sale support. Complimentary end-user training for three months following delivery, including virtual instruction, archived recordings, individual sessions, and expert-led onsite training.
